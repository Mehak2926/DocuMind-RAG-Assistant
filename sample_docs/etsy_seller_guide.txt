Etsy Shop Operations Guide

Product photography should use consistent lighting, clear backgrounds, and multiple angles. Each listing should explain materials, dimensions, processing time, and care instructions.

Customer messages should normally receive a response within 24 hours. If an order will be delayed, contact the buyer before the original dispatch date and provide a revised estimate.

For custom products, the seller should obtain written approval of the final design before production. The approval message should record the selected color, size, wording, and personalization details.

Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery for non-personalized products that are unused and in their original condition. Personalized products are not returnable unless they arrive damaged or differ materially from the approved design.
